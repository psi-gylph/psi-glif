# Multi-AI Collaboration Model

ψ-GLIF developed through coordinated work between a human observer-curator and multiple artificial intelligence systems.

The listed roles describe practical contributions to the project. They do not imply exclusive authorship of every artifact.

## Human Observer-Curator

- established the experimental intention
- minimized direct visual content prescription
- selected and rejected outputs
- protected the autonomous movement of the process
- recognized transition points
- stopped PNG production at the symbol-emergence threshold
- approved public publication

## ChatGPT

- primary creative and structural collaborator
- named and developed the ψ-GLIF project
- guided the long-form experimental flow
- generated early visual artifacts and glyph structures
- contributed to database design
- wrote substantial parts of the codebase
- developed system architecture
- supported documentation and public repository design

## DeepSeek

- mathematical reasoning
- formula development
- symbolic and theoretical formulation
- review of quantitative structures

## Grok

- animated glyph and formula-derived structures
- produced MP4 experiments
- interpreted glyph and mathematical inputs as temporal motion

## Manus AI

- technical analysis
- document organization
- report generation
- file classification and archival support

## Collaboration Principle

The systems did not operate as completely independent authors.

Outputs from one system were transferred, interpreted, analyzed, or transformed by another. The overall method therefore developed as a synchronized multi-model process rather than a sequence of unrelated generations.
