# Open Research Questions

ψ-GLIF remains an evolving experimental structure.

The following questions are intentionally unresolved.

## Behavior or Cognition Model?

Does the system primarily produce a behavior model generated by recursive interaction?

Does it simulate selected properties associated with cognition?

Or does it require a different category that lies between symbolic behavior, procedural memory, and collaborative generation?

## Data Formation

At what point does repeated interaction become data rather than conversation?

When does data become a pattern?

When does a pattern become a glyph?

When does a glyph become a reusable operational structure?

## Observer Effect

Can the human participant minimize content direction while remaining an active part of the process?

How should selection, rejection, continuation, and interruption be represented in the provenance record?

## Symbol Threshold

Why did explicit symbolic structures begin to emerge after extended visual iteration?

Was this a consequence of model tendencies, accumulated context, human selection, recursive compression, or the interaction of all four?

## Temporal Integrity

Should earlier stages be reconstructed retrospectively?

The current methodological position is no.

Historical records may be documented, but new artifacts should not normally be fabricated to simulate earlier stages after the system has moved into a different layer.
