## Methodological Note

The project did not begin with the design of a predetermined image. It began with a shared operational template derived from known principles of human and artificial information processing: input, attention, repetition, compression, association, memory, feedback, and transformation.

The human participant acted as an observer-curator. Direct, copied, illustrative, or conventionally symbolic images were excluded. PNG production was stopped when explicit symbols began to dominate the visual process.
